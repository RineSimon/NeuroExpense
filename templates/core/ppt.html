01

Problem Statement

Managing personal finances is a common challenge. Most individuals face the following issues:

  • Lack of Visibility: Difficulty in tracking small daily expenses, leading to "leaking" money.
  • Complex Tools: Existing financial software is often too complex for average users, requiring manual spreadsheet entry.
  • Inconsistent Budgeting: No real-time alerts when spending exceeds planned limits.
  • Data Fragmentation: Records are often scattered across bank apps, receipts, and memory.
02

Methodology

Agile Development

The project followed an iterative development process, focusing on delivering a Minimum Viable Product (MVP) first and then refining based on user feedback.

User-Centric Design

Prioritizing ease-of-use (UX) and visual appeal (UI) to ensure high user retention and data entry consistency.

Data-Driven Logic

Implementing real-time aggregation and calculation engines to provide instantaneous feedback to the user.

Security-First

Using standard authentication protocols and encrypted data storage to protect sensitive financial information.
