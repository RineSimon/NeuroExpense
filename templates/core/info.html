What is SmartExp?

SmartExp is your personal financial companion. It's not just a list of expenses; it's a smart system designed to help you understand where your money goes, stay within your limits, and make better financial decisions without needing a degree in finance.

Crystal Clear Visibility

Stop wondering where your money went at the end of the month. See your spending broken down by categories like Food, Shopping, and Bills in real-time.

Zero Overspending

Set monthly limits for specific categories. Our smart progress bars change color to warn you when you're getting close to your limit.

Common Questions (Q&A)

Is my data secure?

Absolutely. We use professional-grade security measures to ensure that only you can access your financial records. Your privacy is our top priority.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes! SmartExp is built with a "mobile-first" approach, meaning it looks and works great whether you're on a computer, a tablet, or your smartphone.

Can I export my data for my accountant?

Yes, you can download a professional PDF summary or a clean Excel-ready CSV file with just one click in the Reports section.

Does it support different currencies?

Yes, you can go to the Settings page and choose your preferred currency symbol (like ₹, $, or €) to match your local needs.
